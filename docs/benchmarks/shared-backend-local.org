* Recovery and lifecycle qualification

turso: serial historical recovery wall throughput ranged from 699 to 45213 operations/s across the 12 cases.
duckdb: serial historical recovery wall throughput ranged from 786 to 3032 operations/s across the 12 cases.
Recovery is serial after reopen; the lane count in its raw rows describes the
preceding write workload. It includes every warmup and measured historical
operation and requires equality with the original committed revision. It does
not establish fresh authorization or concurrent recovery throughput.

Both native tests passed. Across 24 cases: 4,320 fresh commits, 4,320 exact
historical recoveries, zero errors and 48 closed Backend instances. Each shutdown
is called twice; the terminal state must be Closed with zero active writes,
zero active recoveries, zero retained request bytes and the exact completed-worker
count. Shutdown timings are for an idle engine, not a busy drain or native RSS
release guarantee. Default-feature source policy and strict all-feature/all-target
Clippy also passed locally.
